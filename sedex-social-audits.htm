Core Social Audit

SEDEX SMETA 2-Pillar

Focuses on core workplace conditions and worker welfare required by international retail buyers:

  • Pillar 1 — Labour Standards: Fair wages, overtime rules, working hours, freedom of association, and zero child/forced labour.
  • Pillar 2 — Health & Safety: Fire safety, machine guarding, electrical safety, PPE, first-aid, and emergency evacuation.

Most Requested by Global Buyers

SEDEX SMETA 4-Pillar

Includes both Labour Standards and Health & Safety plus two additional sustainability pillars:

  • Pillar 3 — Environment (Extended): Pollution control NOCs, hazardous waste disposal, water/energy tracking, and chemical storage.
  • Pillar 4 — Business Ethics: Anti-bribery, anti-corruption, whistleblower policy, and ethical sourcing governance.

Certified Social Standards

SA8000 & WRAP

Formal third-party social accountability certifications valid across global apparel, textile, footwear, and consumer goods supply chains:

  • SA8000: Auditable management system standard based on ILO conventions and UN human rights norms.
  • WRAP: Worldwide Responsible Accredited Production certification widely required by US apparel & textile importers.

How We Prepare Your Factory for SEDEX SMETA

Practical shop-floor, HR, and EHS compliance support from registration to final report upload.

Step 01

SEDEX Advance Registration & SAQ

Setting up your SEDEX Advance company & site profile (ZC/ZS numbers), linking buyer relationships, and completing the Self-Assessment Questionnaire (SAQ).

Step 02

On-Site Factory Gap Inspection

Physical walkthrough of production floors, boiler/chemical areas, dormitories, canteens, emergency exits, and fire-fighting systems against SMETA 7.0 criteria.

Step 03

HR, Payroll & Statutory File Audit

Verification of time-cards, minimum wage registers, overtime calculations, PF/ESIC challans, contractor licenses (CLRA), and age-proof files.

Step 04

Policies, Committees & Worker Training

Forming Health & Safety and POSH committees, conducting fire evacuation drills, and preparing workers for confidential auditor interviews.

Step 05

APSCA Audit Firm Coordination

Coordinating audit scheduling with buyer-approved APSCA member audit bodies (SGS, Intertek, Bureau Veritas, TUV, UL, DNV) and supporting your team on audit day.

Step 06

CAPR Closure & Buyer Approval

Helping submit corrective action evidence (CAPR) on the SEDEX platform so your report is closed cleanly and approved by your international buyers.
