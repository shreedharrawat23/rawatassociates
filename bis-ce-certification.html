Indian Domestic Market

BIS Certification (ISI Mark)

Guidance for Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) product licensing under relevant Indian Standards (IS). Covers in-house testing lab setup, Quality Control Order (QCO) applicability checks, sample dispatch to BIS-recognized labs, and factory inspection readiness.

Crucial for manufacturers of regulated steel/pipes, electricals, chemicals, safety gear, and industrial products sold in India.

European Export Conformity

CE Marking

Technical conformity support for exporting products to the European Economic Area (EEA). Includes applicable EU directive/regulation identification, Essential Health & Safety Requirements (EHSR), Technical Construction File (TCF), risk assessment, and Notified Body coordination where required.

Essential for industrial machinery, electrical equipment, construction products, and export engineering goods.
