FSC-STD-40-004

What is FSC Chain of Custody (CoC)?

Forest Stewardship Council (FSC) Chain of Custody certification verifies that forest-based materials (wood, kraft paper, decor paper, pulp, bamboo) used in your products come from responsibly managed forests and controlled sources.

  • Mandatory for supplying laminates, plywood, and packaging to EU, UK, and US buyers.
  • Covers Transfer, Percentage, and Credit volume accounting systems.
  • Includes full FSC Trademark usage approval and Core Labour Requirements compliance.
EU Regulation 2023/1115

What is EUDR Compliance?

The European Union Deforestation Regulation (EUDR) requires exporters placing wood, paper, furniture, rubber, coffee, cocoa, or soy products on the EU market to prove their products are 100% deforestation-free after December 31, 2020.

  • Plot-level GPS polygon geolocation mapping of raw material harvest origins.
  • Due Diligence Statement (DDS) preparation for EU TRACES NT customs clearance.
  • Prevents EU customs rejection, shipment detention, and heavy financial penalties.
