How We Prepare Your Plant for BRCGS / FSSC 22000

1

Plant Layout & Zoning Gap Audit

Inspection of raw material receiving, high-care/low-risk product flow, personnel hygiene stations, pest control, glass/brittle plastic register, and allergen segregation.

2

HACCP, VACCP & TACCP Study

Complete hazard analysis, CCP/OPRP validation, vulnerability assessment for food fraud (VACCP), and food defence threat plan (TACCP).

3

Traceability Mock Recall & Lab Testing

Conducting 4-hour forward & backward batch traceability drills, mass balance verification, and NABL lab testing (water, swab, shelf-life, residue).

4

Certification Audit & NC Closure

Full support during the accredited Certification Body audit (SGS, TUV, Intertek, Bureau Veritas, DNV) and root-cause closure for top BRCGS grades.

Audit Checklist

Essential Documents Required

  • Valid FSSAI License & Plant Blueprint: Central/State FSSAI license, process flow diagram, and factory zoning layout.
  • NABL Lab Test Reports: Potable water analysis, product microbiological/chemical testing, and environmental hygiene swabs.
  • Calibration & Pest Control Records: Metal detector/CCP calibration certificates and licensed pest control trend reports.
  • Medical & Hygiene Logs: Food handler medical fitness certificates, FoSTaC training certificates, and sanitation SOP logs.
