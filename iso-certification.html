Core ISO Portfolio

ISO Standards We Implement & Certify

Choose standalone certification or combine multiple standards into a cost-effective Integrated Management System (IMS).

Quality (QMS)

ISO 9001:2015

The global benchmark for Quality Management Systems. Builds consistent product/service quality, process control, and customer satisfaction.

Best for: GeM & government tenders, PSU vendor registration, manufacturing, EPC, and trading businesses.

Inquire for ISO 9001
Environment (EMS)

ISO 14001:2015

Establishes an Environmental Management System to control emissions, effluent/waste management, lifecycle impacts, and legal compliance.

Best for: Factories, infrastructure/EPC projects, chemical/composite units, and export buyers requiring green compliance.

Inquire for ISO 14001
Health & Safety (OHSMS)

ISO 45001:2018

Replaces OHSAS 18001. Focuses on hazard identification (HIRA), accident prevention, PPE discipline, and worker safety across active sites.

Best for: Construction, pipelines, power/cables, heavy engineering, and multi-shift manufacturing plants.

Inquire for ISO 45001
Energy (EnMS)

ISO 50001:2018

Provides a structured framework to track energy baselines, Significant Energy Uses (SEUs), power factor/efficiency gains, and carbon reduction.

Best for: Power-intensive industries, cables, steel, cement, cold storages, and ESG sustainability reporting.

Inquire for ISO 50001
InfoSec (ISMS)

ISO/IEC 27001:2022

Protects information confidentiality, integrity, and availability through risk assessment, access controls, backup/continuity, and Annex A controls.

Best for: IT/SaaS companies, BPOs, fintech, security printing/holograms, and enterprise data handling.

Inquire for ISO 27001
Automotive

IATF 16949

Specialized automotive quality standard incorporating core tools (APQP, PPAP, FMEA, MSA, SPC) focused on defect prevention and supply chain consistency.

Best for: OEM suppliers, Tier-1/Tier-2 auto component manufacturers, wiring/cables, and precision engineering units.

Inquire for IATF 16949
Implementation Roadmap

How We Get Your ISO Certified

1

Scope Definition & Gap Assessment

We define your certification scope, sites, processes, and select the right accreditation body (IAF, UKAS, NABCB) based on your tender or export goal.

2

Custom Documentation & SOP Setup

We prepare your quality/safety/environment/ISMS manual, SOPs, risk registers, and records aligned with your real shop-floor or office operations.

3

Internal Audit & Management Review

Our lead auditors conduct internal audit readiness checks, staff awareness guidance, and pre-audit verification to close any gaps early.

4

Stage 1 & Stage 2 Certification Audit

Smooth coordination through documentation and on-site/remote certification audits until your verified ISO certificate is issued.

Basic Documents Needed to Start

  • Business Registration: GST Certificate, Certificate of Incorporation / Partnership Deed, or Udyam/MSME registration.
